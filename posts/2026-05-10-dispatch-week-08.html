NAOMS engineering update · Week 8

Stray test processes can no longer pile up and strangle your machine.

Here's the honest week: the wins are stability, not features. Runaway test processes can no longer pile up and choke your machine — small, hard, and real.

Process Dispatch free May 10, 2026·1 min read·meta

Some weeks you ship features. This week we shipped stability — small, hard, and real.

Shipped

  • Fixed — Stray test processes can no longer pile up and strangle your machine. Three causes closed in one landing: a classifier that silently ignored orphaned test processes because they looked like the real one, a watchdog with no supervisor to restart it after it died, and a test process that could hijack the production socket by defaulting to the real data directory. Shipped — closed.
