Broke & fixed (honest)

  • The revert-storm of 03-19. A dashboard "inspired" tab and an agent-session-bootstrap test suite got reverted, re-reverted, and re-landed in a four-commit flip-flop — the tab pulled, the tests pulled, the test-revert itself reverted (re-landing them), and the tab re-landed. Honest churn — not a production incident, just a visible wobble on a busy day.
  • The bypass we removed. Mid-week we'd drilled a cherry-pick bypass into the merge gate to keep work flowing, then hardened it. We tore it out (a "merge gate rewrite — ff-only, no cherry-pick, no bypass"), and flipped unknown-case guards to deny-by-default ("unknown workflow guards now fail-closed"). Lesson: a gate you have to bypass isn't a gate.

Concept of the week (≤200 words)

"Adopt protocols, skip blockchains." Late in the week NAOMS studied the heavy ledger/identity ecosystems and made a deliberate call — take the good protocol ideas, reject global consensus. You can read the decision in the commit titles themselves: "Hyperledger inspiration — adopt protocols, skip blockchains" and "blockchain sovereignty analysis + GNU Taler inspiration." The reasoning: a global blockchain buys you trustless agreement at the cost of every participant depending on a shared global state — which is exactly the dependency a local-first, sovereign system refuses (the Wholeness axiom). So NAOMS takes the cryptographic and identity primitives these projects pioneered, and leaves the global ledger behind. Honoring the work, choosing a different trade.

From the workshop

The Completion Illusion

At the end of NAOMS's very first week, a signed milestone note read, verbatim: the analysis feels like the fix — it's not; a pattern without a policy is a belief without enforcement. It names the trap we still fall into weekly: understanding a problem hands your brain the same reward as solving it, so you stop one step short. A folder full of brilliant design looks exactly like progress — which makes it the most dangerous kind of completion illusion there is. We've learned to treat that click of clarity as a halfway signal, not a finish line. The unglamorous implement-step is where a belief becomes mechanical reality instead of merely something you sincerely meant.
