Your networks, one honest inbox

Messages from your outside networks now arrive in one calm place in NAOMS — a note from Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, or a Matrix room shows up as a real chat bubble tagged with where it truly came from. One honest limit up front: today this is where your networks arrive, not yet where you reply back out to them.

Product Architect free July 25, 2026·3 min read
TL;DR Messages from your outside networks now land in one quiet place inside NAOMS. A note from Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, or a Matrix room arrives as a real chat bubble, each one tagged with where it truly came from, with Matrix the one we have proven end to end. Two honest limits: today you can watch your networks arrive but cannot yet reply out to them from here, and one network — Signal — is built but not connected yet, so it shows a blocked state instead of pretending. And when you add someone to a shared conversation, they can now sign and take part immediately, as a real participant.

Your outside world does not live in one place. A friend messages you on one network, a group lives on another, a room you follow is on a third. You keep the map in your head and pay a small tax every time you go looking.

We wanted the opposite: one honest place where your networks arrive. Open chat in NAOMS today and a message from an outside network shows up as a real chat bubble, sitting in the same calm surface as everything else — not a separate app you leave to go check, not a mirror you have to keep refreshing. It simply arrives.

One place where your networks arrive

When a message comes in from one of your connected networks, it lands here as an ordinary chat bubble. A note from a Telegram chat, a message from a WhatsApp thread, something said in a Discord channel, a line from a Matrix room — they all flow into the same quiet list you already read the rest of your conversations in. You stop switching apps to find who said what. It comes to you.

The one we have proven all the way through, end to end, is Matrix — a message from a Matrix room arrives and renders here as the real thing, and we have verified that path from the outside network to your bubble. The others arrive and render too; Matrix is the one we can stand behind as fully proven.

Every bubble tells you where it truly came from

We were strict about one thing. Every message that arrives keeps an honest tag of where it actually came from — "via Telegram," "via WhatsApp," "via Discord," "via Matrix." We never rewrite a message's origin to make the list look tidier. Calm is worth a lot; it is not worth pretending a Discord message was something else. The tag is the truth of the message's journey, and it stays with the bubble.

Honest about what is still being wired

Here is the part we will not dress up, because it is the whole point.

Today, this is where your networks arrive — not yet where you reply. You can watch your outside conversations land in one calm place and read them in context, but you cannot yet send a message back out to those networks from here. The sending side is what we are building next. We would rather tell you that plainly than let the surface imply a two-way chat it does not yet do.

And one network is honest in a second way. Signal is built, but it is not connected yet — so instead of showing you a fake, cheerful thread, it shows a blocked state and says so out loud. An empty room you can trust beats a crowded one you cannot. When Signal is truly connected, it will arrive like the rest; until then, it will not pretend.

So the promise we can stand behind today is this: one honest place where your networks arrive, each message a real bubble tagged with where it truly came from, Matrix proven end to end. Not "everyone, everywhere, both directions, instantly." A real, quiet surface you can trust — that grows as we wire the sending side and bring each remaining network online.

And when you bring someone in, they are a real participant

One more thing shipped alongside this, quietly important. When you add someone to a shared conversation, they now get their own ability to sign and take part the moment they join. They are not a guest peering in over your shoulder and not waiting on anyone to grant them a voice — they join as a full participant, able to sign for themselves right away. Bringing a person in actually brings them in.
