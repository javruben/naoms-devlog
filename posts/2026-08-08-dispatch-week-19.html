Dispatch · Week 19: An Agent in the Room, on Your Terms

An AI can now sit in a human chat channel only with everyone's consent, on a scope you granted and can audit — and a run of canvas-window fixes landed on the parts your hands touch every day.

Process Dispatch free August 8, 2026·2 min read·meta
TL;DR Week 19 was about trust. An AI can now join a human chat channel only with consent from the people in it, limited to the exact tools you granted, with a participation history any normal user can read — sovereignty over the agent in the room. Alongside it, a run of canvas-window bugs closed on the surfaces you use every day.

This week's headline is a feature you'll actually touch — letting an AI into a conversation without letting it quietly become a member.

By the numbers

  • ~702 commits in the window 08-03 → 08-08 (227 merge, ~475 non-merge). A calmer week than the last by volume.

Sovereignty over the agent in the room

The user-facing headline, and the one most in keeping with what this project is for. You can now bring an AI into a human chat channel — but only on terms everyone in the room, and you in particular, actually set.

The pieces that make that real: the chooser lists only agents you've actually delegated to; a refused invite tells you the real reason rather than a generic error; admitting an agent is a personal-sovereignty decision that no channel's governance model can override; the tools it gets are exactly the ones consented at approval, minted into a real capability token and checked on every single call at execution time; a cross-person agent has to ask for information rather than silently see it; and the whole participation history — who granted, who objected, what the agent did — is a read-only panel a normal user can open. A joined agent is visible and revocable from the shipping interface. This landed celebrated (close-gate signed, 2026-08-04). The through-line: an agent is a guest with papers, not a fixture that quietly acquires the run of the house.

The surface didn't stop either

The things you look at kept getting fixed. A run of canvas-window bugs closed — a stacked window now maximizes to a flush layout instead of zooming the whole deck, the first AI chat opens docked rather than floating, search boxes grow without jumping, no stray text gets selected mid-resize, and a broken create-channel dialog layout was repaired. Unglamorous, exactly-right polish on the parts your hands touch every day.

The honest ledger

Told straight, by status:

  • Shipped and celebrated (close-gate signed): consent-governed AI participation in human chat channels, with granted-tool scope, per-call enforcement, and a readable audit trail.
  • Landed on main: a batch of canvas-window bug fixes.
  • Live on main, honestly in-progress: chat-protocol parity for bridged conversations.

We mark these on purpose. Celebrate what's real; never imply the partial is finished.
